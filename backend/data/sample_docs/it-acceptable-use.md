# IT Acceptable Use Policy

## Passwords and Access
Passwords must be at least 14 characters and changed every 180 days. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all company systems. Never share your credentials with anyone, including IT staff.

## Devices
Company laptops must have disk encryption enabled and automatic screen lock after 5 minutes of inactivity. Personal devices may not store customer data.

## Reporting Incidents
Report lost devices or suspected security incidents to the IT Security desk within 1 hour of discovery.
