# New Joiner Onboarding Guide

## Before Day One
New joiners must submit a government ID, signed offer letter and bank details to HR at least 3 working days before the start date.

## First Week
On day one you will receive your laptop, badge and system accounts. A buddy is assigned to every new joiner for the first 30 days. Mandatory security and compliance training must be completed within 7 days of joining.

## Probation
The standard probation period is 90 days. A review meeting with your manager takes place at day 45 and day 90.
