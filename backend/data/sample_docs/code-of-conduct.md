# Code of Conduct

## Respectful Workplace
All employees must treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect. Harassment, discrimination and bullying are not tolerated and may result in disciplinary action up to termination.

## Gifts and Hospitality
Employees must not accept gifts worth more than 50 USD from vendors or partners. Any gift above this value must be declined or reported to the Compliance team within 5 working days.

## Reporting Concerns
Concerns can be reported to your manager, HR, or anonymously through the ethics hotline. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is strictly prohibited.
