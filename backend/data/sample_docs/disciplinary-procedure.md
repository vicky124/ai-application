# Disciplinary Procedure

## Stages
The procedure has three stages: a verbal warning, a written warning valid for 12 months, and a final written warning valid for 24 months. Gross misconduct may result in immediate termination after an investigation.

## Investigation
HR assigns an investigator within 3 working days of a formal complaint. The employee must receive written notice of the allegations at least 48 hours before any hearing.
