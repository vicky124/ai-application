# Leave Policy

## Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly at 2 days per month. Up to 5 unused days may be carried forward to the next year; any remaining days expire on 31 March.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 2 consecutive days. Sick leave cannot be carried forward.

## Parental Leave
Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers are entitled to 4 weeks of paid parental leave. Leave must be taken within 12 months of birth or adoption.

## How to Apply
Submit leave requests through the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance for annual leave. Your manager approves or declines the request within 2 working days. Sick leave should be reported to your manager before the start of the working day.
