# Salary Bands

## Bands
Engineer I earns 60,000 to 80,000 USD. Engineer II earns 80,000 to 110,000 USD. Senior Engineer earns 110,000 to 150,000 USD.

## Bonus
The annual bonus target is 10 percent of base salary for bands up to Senior Engineer and 20 percent for Director and above.

## Review
Bands are reviewed every January by HR and Finance.
