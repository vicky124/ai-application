# Forms and Codes Reference

## Forms
Form HR-204 is used to request parental leave. Form FIN-17 is the expense claim form. Form IT-88 is used to request new software.

## Policy Codes
Policy code SEC-9 covers removable media such as USB drives. Policy code PRV-3 covers retention of customer records.

## Cost Centres
Cost centre 4410 is used for training and conference expenses. Cost centre 5120 is used for recruitment agency fees.
