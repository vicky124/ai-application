# Employee Benefits Guide

## Health Insurance
Medical, dental and vision insurance covers the employee, spouse and dependent children from the first day of employment.

## Retirement
The company matches retirement contributions up to 5 percent of base salary after 6 months of service.

## Learning Budget
Each employee has a learning budget of 1,000 USD per calendar year for courses, books and conferences. Manager approval is required for anything above 500 USD.

## Wellness
A wellness stipend of 30 USD per month is paid for gym memberships or fitness apps. An annual health check is covered in full.

## Employee Assistance
A confidential counselling service is available 24 hours a day at no cost to employees and their families.
