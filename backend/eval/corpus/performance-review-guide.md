# Performance Review Guide

## Cycle
Performance reviews take place twice a year, in June and December. Ratings use a scale of 1 to 5 where 3 means meets expectations.

## Preparation
Employees submit a self-assessment at least 7 days before the review meeting. Managers collect feedback from at least 3 peers.

## Calibration
HR runs calibration sessions so that ratings are consistent across teams. Final ratings are shared with employees within 10 working days.

## Improvement Plans
An employee rated 1 or 2 may be placed on a 60-day performance improvement plan with clear goals and weekly check-ins.
