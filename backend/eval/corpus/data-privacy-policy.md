# Data Privacy Policy

## Data Classification
Company data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Customer personal data is always Confidential or higher.

## Retention
Personal data is kept for 24 months after a customer relationship ends and is then deleted or anonymised.

## Breach Reporting
Any suspected personal data breach must be reported to the Data Protection Officer within 24 hours of discovery. The company notifies regulators within 72 hours when required by law.

## Individual Rights
Customers may request access to or deletion of their personal data. Requests must be answered within 30 days.

## Sharing
Personal data may only be shared with approved vendors that have signed a data processing agreement.
