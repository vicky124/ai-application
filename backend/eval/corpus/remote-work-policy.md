# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week after completing the 90-day probation period. Managers approve the remote schedule.

## Core Hours
Remote employees must be available during core hours of 10:00 to 16:00 in their local time zone.

## Equipment and Allowance
The company provides a laptop and a monitor. A home-office allowance of 150 USD per month covers internet and utilities.

## Security
Use the company VPN and multi-factor authentication whenever working outside the office. Never work on customer data from public Wi-Fi without the VPN.

## Working Abroad
Working from another country for more than 14 days per year requires approval from HR and Legal because of tax and visa rules.
