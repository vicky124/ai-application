# Travel and Expense Policy

## Meals
Employees may claim up to 40 USD per day for meals while travelling. A receipt is required for every claim above 10 USD. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Flights and Hotels
Book economy flights at least 14 days before departure. Business class requires VP approval for flights longer than 6 hours. Hotel stays are capped at 180 USD per night, or 250 USD per night in New York, London and Tokyo.

## Submitting Claims
Submit expense claims within 30 days of the trip through the finance portal. Claims older than 60 days are rejected. Finance reimburses approved claims within 10 working days.

## Mileage
Use of a personal vehicle for business travel is reimbursed at 0.58 USD per mile.
