# Whistleblower Policy

## Raising a Concern
Employees can report suspected wrongdoing through the ethics hotline, to Compliance, or to any senior manager. Reports may be made anonymously.

## Investigation
Compliance acknowledges every report within 3 working days and aims to complete the investigation within 45 days.

## Protection
Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is prohibited and may lead to dismissal.
